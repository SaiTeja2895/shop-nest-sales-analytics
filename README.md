# Shop Nest Sales Analytics

## Project Overview

Shop Nest Sales Analytics is an end-to-end e-commerce data analytics project focused on understanding sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, profitability, and order operations.

The project demonstrates a practical workflow from raw data preparation and SQL analysis to an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Understand customer segment contribution
- Track sales trends over time
- Analyze order status and operational performance
- Evaluate discounts and profitability
- Provide business insights through an interactive dashboard

## Dashboard Analysis

### Sales Over Time

Tracks monthly sales performance and helps identify changes and patterns in revenue over the selected period.

### Top 10 Products by Sales

Highlights the products generating the highest sales and helps identify key products contributing to overall business performance.

### Sales by Category

Compares sales contribution across product categories to identify strong and weaker-performing categories.

### Profit by Category

Shows category-level profitability and helps evaluate which categories contribute most to profit.

### Orders by Status

Provides visibility into order outcomes and supports operational analysis of completed and non-completed orders.

### Sales by Customer Segment

Compares sales contribution across customer segments and helps identify the most valuable customer groups.

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard is designed with filters for:

- Category
- Payment Method
- Order Status
- Customer Segment

A reset option is also included to return the dashboard to the default view.
